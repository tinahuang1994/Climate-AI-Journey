# Prompting Like a Pro: Google's 5-Step Framework

*Created: March 2026 | Source: Google Prompting Essentials (Coursera)*

Hey there! Ready to turn your AI into a high-performing partner? Based on Google's **"Prompting Essentials"** framework, getting great results isn't about magic---it's about a simple, repeatable process.

Think of an AI like a very talented intern: they can do almost anything, but they need clear directions. Here is the **5-Step Framework** Google teaches to help you prompt like a pro.

**🛠 The 5-Step Prompting Framework**

**1. Task (The "What")**

Clearly state what you want the AI to do. Use an **action verb** (Write, Summarize, Analyze, Draft).

-   **Case Study:** Instead of saying "Help with a trip," say **"Create a 3-day travel itinerary for a family trip to Tokyo."**

**2. Context (The "Who & Why")**

Give the AI a "Persona" and background info. Who is the AI supposed to be? Who is the audience?

-   **Case Study:** Add to your prompt: **"Act as a professional travel agent. The family includes two toddlers, so the pace should be slow with plenty of kid-friendly stops."**

**3. References (The "How")**

Provide examples or specific data to guide the style or facts. This is where you give it "source material" to work from.

-   **Case Study:** Add: **"Focus the itinerary on the Shibuya and Asakusa areas, similar to the relaxed style of this blog post \[Link/Text\]."**

**4. Evaluate (The "Check")**

Look at the result and ask: *Is this what I wanted?* Google emphasizes checking for **bias** and **hallucinations** (made-up facts).

-   **Case Study:** You notice the AI suggested a 10:00 PM dinner. Since you have toddlers, that won't work.

**5. Iterate (The "Fix")**

Don't start over! Just tell the AI what to change. This is the "secret sauce" of pro prompting.

-   **Case Study:** Tell the AI: **"This is great, but move all dinners to 5:30 PM and suggest one indoor play area for a rainy day backup."**

**🚀 Pro Tips for Refining**

If the 5 steps don't get you there immediately, Google suggests these **4 Iteration Methods**:

-   **Separate Tasks:** Break one giant prompt into 3 smaller, linked steps.

-   **Introduce Constraints:** "Use no more than 200 words" or "Format this as a table."

-   **Try Different Phrasing:** If "Summarize" is too dry, try "Explain this like I'm five."

-   **Few-Shot Prompting:** Give the AI 2 or 3 examples of the exact style you want before asking for the final result.

**Note:** Always remember---the quality of your prompt defines the quality of your output. Treat it like a conversation!
